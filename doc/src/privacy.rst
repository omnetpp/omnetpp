==============
Privacy Notice
==============

This site, the OMNeT++ documentation, sets no cookies and sends nothing about you anywhere unless you agree to it in
the cookie consent dialog. The dialog appears on your first visit; to change your choice later, click
`Cookie settings <#__consent>`__ here or at the bottom of any page.

Google Analytics
----------------

If you accept it, the site uses `Google Analytics <https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/>`__ to count
visits and see which pages are read, so that we know which parts of the documentation to improve. Google Analytics
sets the ``_ga`` and ``_ga_QGGDS2R96M`` cookies, and sends the address of the pages you visit, your browser and device
type, and your approximate location to Google. See Google's `privacy policy <https://policies.google.com/privacy>`__
for how Google handles this data.

GitHub
------

If you accept it, the header shows the number of stars and forks of the
`OMNeT++ repository <https://github.com/omnetpp/omnetpp>`__, which your browser fetches from GitHub. GitHub sees your
IP address in that request; see the `GitHub privacy statement
<https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>`__.

Settings stored in your browser
-------------------------------

The site remembers your choices in your browser's local storage: your answer to the cookie consent dialog, the color
scheme, and the widths of the sidebars that you set. They never leave your browser.
